Textile Longevity

Handloom Saree Care Guide

Everyday Mulmul Cotton

Gentle hand wash in cold water using mild, eco-friendly liquid soap. Never wring or spin. Dry in gentle shade to preserve color saturation. Warm iron on reverse side.

Sungudi Cotton

Sungudi sarees carry natural vegetable dyes. For the first wash, dry clean recommended or soak in salt water for 5 minutes. Store rolled or neatly folded.

Narayani Pattu (Pure Silk)

Always dry clean only. Wrap in breathable unbleached muslin cloth or a soft cotton bag. Never store in plastic. Air the saree once every six months in indirect breeze.
