Chapter 01 · The Lineage

The rhythm of the handloom.

KADHA began with a simple observation: modern wardrobes often celebrate speed, but our most cherished memories are attached to things made with care.

We work directly with weaver collectives across South India and Bengal, preserving centuries-old pit loom techniques, tie-resist Sungudi dyeing, and fine mulmul spinning. No conveyor belts, no synthetic shortcuts—just skilled hands and natural threads.

Chapter 02 · Honest Materials

Pure fibres, living drapes.

Our cottons are breathable, soft against the skin, and designed to soften with every wash. Our silks are woven from unadulterated yarn with real zari motifs that will be passed down to daughters and granddaughters.

We only produce in limited, intentional batches. When an edition sells out, our weavers begin anew, ensuring fair compensation, humane working hours, and zero inventory waste.
