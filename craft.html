The Beauty of the Process

A little more human.

Every thread has a maker. Every detail has a reason. Discover the time-honoured stages of weaving a genuine KADHA drape.

From Fibre to Heirloom

Four stages of the loom.

STAGE 01 · YARN & DYE

Natural Cotton & Silk

Hand-selected cotton and unweighted mulberry silk are washed and dipped in small earthen vats using heritage plant and mineral dyes.

STAGE 02 · RESIST & IKAT

Tied by Hand

For our Sungudi and Ikat collections, threads are counted and tied individually before immersion, creating signature soft-edged geometric motifs.

STAGE 03 · THE PIT LOOM

Patience in Every Pass

The weaver coordinates foot pedals and a wooden flying shuttle in a rhythmic cadence, producing only 1 to 2 metres per full working day.

STAGE 04 · ZARI & TASSLES

Heirloom Borders

Intricate temple borders and hand-knotted fringe tassels are hand-finished to ensure longevity across decades of wear.
